<div data-lang="zh">

Z2Y 记时在 App Store 更新至 **2.0.0**。本次更新聚焦三个板块的交互体验：计时的方案列表与运行页更好用，记时可以删除不再需要的计划与纪念日，专注的进入与退出更顺。

完成一段之后依然会留下一枚印记，时间簿的日页与周页对所有用户免费；月页日历与年度长期回顾属于可选的 Pro。

完整的界面预览、使用步骤与常见问题见 [Z2Y 记时使用说明](/support/z2y-timer/)，也可以在 [应用目录](/apps/z2y-timer/) 查看当前版本与系统要求。

</div>

<div data-lang="en">

Z2Y Remember Timer is now at **2.0.0** on the App Store. This release focuses on the interaction of its three sections: the countdown plan list and running screen are easier to use, Remember can delete plans and anniversaries you no longer need, and Focus is smoother to enter and leave.

A finished session still leaves a mark, and the day and week pages of the time book stay free for everyone; the month calendar and the yearly long-term review belong to optional Pro.

See the [usage guide](/support/z2y-timer/) for interface previews, step-by-step instructions, and troubleshooting, or open the [app catalog](/apps/z2y-timer/) for the current version and requirements.
