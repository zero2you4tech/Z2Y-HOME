<div data-lang="zh" markdown="1">

Z2Y 强记单词在 App Store 更新至 **1.2.2**。这次更新把词书、单词列表、学习与设置页面的视觉层级和操作路径重新梳理了一遍：提醒间隔（1–3600 秒）保存之后，单词卡按同一节奏出现，卡片下方直接显示下一次提醒的等待时间。

同时新增学习统计与图表：学习进度、学习效率、出现次数最多的 10 个单词与遗忘次数最多的 10 个单词集中在一页；单词列表可以搜索单词或释义、按词频排名排序，并显示每个单词的出现、记住与遗忘次数。iPhone、iPad 与 Mac 版本均已更新到 1.2.2。

当前版本、系统要求与界面预览见 [应用目录](/apps/z2y-words/)。

</div>

<div data-lang="en" markdown="1">

Z2Y Force Remember Words is now at **1.2.2** on the App Store. This release reworks the visual hierarchy and interaction across the book list, word list, study session, and settings: once the reminder interval (1–3600 seconds) is saved, word cards arrive on that rhythm and the countdown to the next reminder sits right below the card.

It also adds study statistics and charts — learning progress, learning efficiency, the top 10 most frequently appeared words, and the top 10 most forgotten words on one page — while the word list searches words or meanings, sorts by frequency rank, and shows appeared, remembered, and forgotten counts for each word. The iPhone, iPad, and Mac versions are all on 1.2.2.

See the [app catalog](/apps/z2y-words/) for the current version, requirements, and interface previews.
