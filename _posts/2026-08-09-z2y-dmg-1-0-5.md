<div data-lang="zh" markdown="1">

Z2Y DMG Plus 在 Mac App Store 更新至 **1.0.5**。这一版把打包流程做成一个完整的发布工作台：选择源 App、文件或文件夹之后，可以输出一键安装布局——用户打开 DMG 里的安装器即完成安装并自动启动——也可以输出经典拖拽布局。

构建前会先检查源 App 的签名与输出位置，构建后给出产物校验结果。每次打包都会写进「构建历史」，可以随时回看配置、结果与产物体积，并重新打开已验证的产物；需要一起分发的说明文档可以附加到 DMG 根目录。界面支持中英文切换，全部处理都在本机完成。

界面预览、使用步骤与常见问题见 [Z2Y DMG Plus 支持页](/support/z2y-dmg/)，当前版本与系统要求可以在 [应用目录](/apps/z2y-dmg/) 查看。

<div data-lang="en" markdown="1">

Z2Y DMG Plus is now at **1.0.5** on the Mac App Store. This release turns packaging into a complete release workbench: after choosing an app, file, or folder you can output a one-click installer layout — the customer opens one app in the DMG and it installs and launches — or the classic drag-to-Applications layout.

The build checks the source signature and destination first and reports the artifact verification afterwards. Every build is written to Build history, where you can revisit its configuration, result, and artifact size and reopen a verified artifact; documents that ship with the DMG can be attached at the DMG root. The interface switches between Chinese and English, and everything runs on your Mac.

See the [Z2Y DMG Plus support page](/support/z2y-dmg/) for interface previews and troubleshooting, or open the [app catalog](/apps/z2y-dmg/) for the current version and requirements.
