<div data-lang="zh" markdown="1">

独立产品最容易犯的错误，是从“我想做一个什么功能”开始。功能很容易被写进清单，但很少能独立解释一个产品为什么值得被长期使用。

我们更愿意从一个真实问题开始：什么事情反复发生，什么地方总在消耗时间，什么工具之间的切换让人无法进入状态？当问题足够具体，产品方向也会自然变得清楚。

Z2Y 的多个产品都来自这种摩擦。请喝水来自一次真实的健康事件；阅读管理来自信息散落；Java 复习来自资料很多但路径不清；DMG 打包来自重复发布流程。

产品不是一次性的答案。它应该随着使用继续暴露问题、改变结构，并把每一次变化记录下来。这也是这个网站存在的意义：不仅展示产品，也展示它们如何被长期建造。

</div>

<div data-lang="en" markdown="1">

The easiest mistake in independent product work is starting with “what feature do I want to build?” Features are easy to list, but they rarely explain why a product deserves long-term use.

We prefer to start with a real problem: what keeps happening, what repeatedly consumes time, and which context switches prevent people from getting into flow? When the problem is specific enough, the product direction becomes clear.

Several Z2Y products came from that kind of friction. Drink Water! came from a real health event. Reader & Manager came from scattered information. Java review came from having lots of material but no clear path. DMG Creator came from a repetitive release process.

A product is not a one-time answer. It should keep exposing problems, changing structure, and recording each change. That is also why this site exists: to show the products and how they are built over time.

</div>
