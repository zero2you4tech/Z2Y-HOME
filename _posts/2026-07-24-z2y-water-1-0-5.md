Z2Y 请喝水已更新至 **1.0.5**。这次重做了饮水提醒界面：主界面用倒计时环显示距离下一次补水还剩多久，并记录今日完成次数；到点后由透明悬浮窗口接管屏幕，至少 10 秒后才能确认离开座位。

设置页也重新整理：提醒间隔最长可设 3600 秒，并按需要打开开机启动，让应用在登录后自动在后台运行。本次更新同时优化了提醒流程与中英文显示，修复了计时状态同步问题。

安装前请确认你能够接受较强的全屏提醒。界面截图与完整说明见 [应用详情](/apps/z2y-water/)。

<div data-lang="en" markdown="1">

Z2Y Drink Water! is now at **1.0.5**. This release rebuilds the hydration reminder interface: the dashboard uses a countdown ring to show how long is left until your next sip and keeps today's total, and when the time is up a transparent floating window takes over the screen — you must wait at least 10 seconds before you can confirm your way out of the seat.

Settings were reorganised too: intervals go up to 3,600 seconds, and you can turn on launch at login so the app comes up quietly in the background after you sign in. The release also improves the reminder flow and the Chinese and English text, and fixes timer state handling.

Please make sure you are comfortable with strong full-screen reminders before installing. See the [app page](/apps/z2y-water/) for screenshots and details.
