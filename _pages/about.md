## 从零到你的产品

Zero2You Studio 是一间独立产品工作室。我们关注成长、知识与效率工具，希望用户在使用产品的过程中获得更清晰的认知、更稳定的习惯，以及更强的创造能力。

我们不追求功能堆叠，而是更在意一个产品是否真的进入日常：它能不能减少一次切换，能不能让一个复杂问题更容易理解，能不能在长期使用中慢慢变好。

## 我们正在做什么

当前产品覆盖 macOS、iOS 与开发工具，包括 AI、Prompt 管理、阅读与知识整理、Java 学习、健康提醒、DMG 打包和内容工作流。

你可以从 [应用目录](/apps/) 查看每个产品的版本、安装方式和支持信息，也可以在 [产品动态](/updates/) 与 [工作室手记](/journal/) 中了解最新进展。

## From zero to your product

Zero2You Studio is an independent product studio focused on growth, knowledge, and productivity tools. We want products to help people gain clearer understanding, build more stable habits, and create with greater confidence.

We are less interested in stacking features than in whether a product becomes part of daily life: does it remove a context switch, make a complex problem easier to understand, and improve over time?

## What we are building

Our products span macOS, iOS, and developer tooling, including AI and prompt management, reading and knowledge organization, Java learning, health reminders, DMG packaging, and content workflows.

Open the [app catalog](/apps/) for versions, installation instructions, and support. Follow [product updates](/updates/) and the [studio journal](/journal/) for current work.
