# Z2Y 养生 · 使用说明与支持

Z2Y 养生是一款围绕传统文化与日常生活的养生应用：养生板块以子午流注为线索，显示当前时辰、对应经络、中医理论、节奏提示与当天可执行的生活建议；食补板块把手边的食材记成一份清单，按现有食材给出养生菜谱，并提供 233 道菜谱与阳台种菜指南。应用不需要注册账号，添加的食材与偏好保存在本机。

Z2Y Health is a wellness app built around traditional culture and everyday life. Its Wellness section follows the meridian-flow clock and shows the current time phase, the corresponding meridian, the related theory, a rhythm cue, and one actionable suggestion for the day. Its Food section keeps an inventory of what you have at home, suggests dishes from those ingredients, and adds a 233-dish recipe collection plus a balcony-gardening guide. No account is required, and the ingredients you add and your preferences stay on your device.

- 平台 Platform：iPhone / iPad，iOS 15.6 或更高版本
- 价格 Price：App Store ¥1.00 / $0.99
- 当前版本 Current version：1.0.1

应用内容为传统文化与生活方式知识，用于学习参考，不提供医疗诊断、健康测量、治疗建议或用药指导，也不能替代专业人士的意见。
The app's content is traditional-culture and lifestyle knowledge for reference. It provides no medical diagnosis, measurements, treatment advice, or medication guidance, and it cannot replace professional advice.

## 快速开始

1. 从 [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/id6751483661) 安装 Z2Y 养生并打开，无需注册或登录。
2. 首次打开会看到中英双语引导，可在右上角切换「中文 / EN」，读完点「开始体验」进入应用。
3. 在「养生」查看当前时辰与刻（初刻 / 一刻 / 二刻 / 三刻）、对应经络、中医理论、节奏提示和一条生活建议；需要完整内容时打开十二时辰对照表。
4. 在「食补」的「家」里添加手边食材（添加第一条后示例食材会自动清除），在「菜单」按分类浏览 233 道菜谱，在「种菜」查看阳台种植方法。

## 三个板块怎么用

### 养生 · 子午流注

- 卡片顶部显示今天日期与当前时辰，并给出该时辰对应的经络（如「小肠经」）与一句中医理论说明。
- 「节奏提示」给出这个时段适合的活动强度与坐姿提醒，「生活建议」给出一条具体可做的事。
- 需要完整内容时可以打开十二时辰完整对照表，按顺序查看每个时辰的经络与作息建议。
- 页面底部固定提示：应用内容仅供学习参考，不用于医疗诊断或治疗。

### 食补 · 家 / 菜单 / 种菜

- **家**：把家里现有的食材记成清单，可以按网格或列表查看，并给出「养生菜谱推荐」的方向；示例食材只用于说明，添加你自己的第一条食物后会自动清除。
- **菜单**：菜谱大全按分类整理（例如「猪肉菜」），每条菜品都带一个播放样式的入口，方便在做饭时查看做法。
- **种菜**：阳台种菜的入门指引，适合在城市环境里从零开始种一点能吃的东西。

### 关于

- 「关于」介绍无何有工作室与我们在做的其他应用，方便从当前应用跳去了解其他产品。

## Getting started

1. Install Z2Y Health from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/id6751483661) and open it. No sign-up or login is required.
2. The first launch opens a bilingual guide; switch between 中文 and EN in the top corner, then tap **Start Experience**.
3. On **Wellness**, review the current time phase and quarter (初刻 / 一刻 / 二刻 / 三刻), its meridian, the related theory, a rhythm cue, and one suggestion for the day; open the twelve-phase table when you want the full list.
4. In **Food**, add what you have at home on **Home** (the sample items clear once you add your own), browse the 233-dish collection by category on **Menu**, and read the balcony-gardening guide on **Grow**.

## How the three sections work

### Wellness · meridian flow

- The card shows today's date and the current time phase, together with that phase's meridian (for example 小肠经, the small intestine meridian) and a one-line note on the related theory.
- **Rhythm cue** describes the activity level and posture that suit this part of the day, and **Daily suggestion** gives one concrete thing to do.
- Open the full twelve-phase table when you want to read each phase in order with its meridian and daily-rhythm advice.
- A fixed note at the bottom of the page states that the content is for reference and is not used for medical diagnosis or treatment.

### Food · Home / Menu / Grow

- **Home** keeps what you already have as an inventory you can view as a grid or a list, and points you at the wellness recipes it can suggest; the sample items are there for illustration only and clear as soon as you add your own first ingredient.
- **Menu** is the recipe collection, grouped by category (such as pork dishes), and every entry opens the cooking method while you are in the kitchen.
- **Grow** is a balcony-gardening primer for starting something edible from scratch in a city home.

### About

- **About** introduces the Zero2You studio and the other apps we build, so you can move from this app to the rest of the catalogue.

## Data and privacy

- No account, no cloud sync, and no ads.
- The ingredients you add, the language, and your interface preferences are stored on your device. Deleting the app or clearing its storage removes them permanently.
- The content is traditional-culture and lifestyle knowledge; it offers no medical diagnosis, measurements, treatment advice, or medication guidance.
