## 找到答案

每个应用详情页都包含当前版本、系统要求、安装步骤、常见问题和版本记录。进入 [应用目录](/apps/) 选择对应产品即可查看。

## 需要人工帮助？

请发送邮件至 [petterobam@gmail.com](mailto:petterobam@gmail.com)，并在邮件中注明：

- 应用名称与版本
- macOS 或 iOS 版本
- 问题发生的完整步骤
- 预期结果与实际结果
- 错误截图或构建日志

请不要发送密码、签名证书、私钥、访问令牌或其他敏感信息。

## 应用专项支持

- [通过 GitHub 提交问题](https://github.com/zero2you4tech)

## Find an answer

Every app detail page includes the current version, requirements, installation steps, FAQs, and release history. Open the [app catalog](/apps/) and choose the product you need.

## Need human help?

Email [petterobam@gmail.com](mailto:petterobam@gmail.com) and include:

- App name and version
- macOS or iOS version
- Exact steps that reproduce the issue
- Expected and actual result
- Error screenshot or build log

Do not send passwords, signing certificates, private keys, access tokens, or other sensitive information.

## App-specific support

- [Z2Y DMG Plus support](/support/z2y-dmg/)
- [Report an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/zero2you4tech)

Support requests are normally reviewed within three business days.
