## 先去支持站，再发邮件

所有公开的问题、需求与已知故障都集中在我们自建的支持站点，方便其他人搜索到同样的答案：

- [中文支持站](https://issue-zh.zero2you.tech/)：报告问题、查看已知问题、提交功能需求。
- [English support site](https://issue-en.zero2you.tech/): report issues, browse known issues, and request features.

在支持站提交时，请附上下面这些信息，处理会快很多。

## 提交问题时请提供

- 应用名称与版本号（在应用详情页或应用「关于」中可以看到）
- macOS 或 iOS 的系统版本
- 问题发生的完整步骤：从哪个界面开始，点了什么
- 预期结果与实际结果
- 错误截图或构建日志

请不要提交密码、签名证书、私钥、访问令牌等敏感信息。

## 应用专项支持

- Z2Y DMG Plus：见 [专项支持页](/support/z2y-dmg/)，或到 [支持站](https://issue-zh.zero2you.tech/) 搜索同类问题。
- 其他应用：先在 [应用目录](/apps/) 查看该应用的说明、系统要求与常见问题。

支持请求通常会在三个工作日内查看。

## Start with the support site

Public issues, feature requests, and known failures live on our own support sites, so other users can find the same answer:

- [English support site](https://issue-en.zero2you.tech/): report an issue, browse known issues, and request features.
- [中文支持站](https://issue-zh.zero2you.tech/)：中文问题与需求入口。

Including the details below makes a report much faster to handle.

## What to include

- App name and version (shown on the app detail page or in the app's About)
- Your macOS or iOS version
- The exact steps that reproduce the issue: which screen you started from and what you clicked
- Expected result and actual result
- Error screenshot or build log

Do not include passwords, signing certificates, private keys, or access tokens.

## App-specific support

- Z2Y DMG Plus: see the [app support page](/support/z2y-dmg/) or search the [support site](https://issue-en.zero2you.tech/).
- Other apps: start from the [app catalog](/apps/) for instructions, requirements, and FAQs.

Support requests are normally reviewed within three business days.
